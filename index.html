Do markets overreact when a star gets hurt?

The thesis: when a star is ruled out, prediction markets and sportsbooks overreact, so the injured team becomes undervalued. If that is true, it should win more often than its post-news price implies and cover the spread more than the 52.4% needed to profit at standard odds.

The motivating case: Miami at Notre Dame, Nov 7 2026

Malachi Toney was ruled out Wednesday, Nov 4. The spread moved from Notre Dame −3.5 at open to −7 and −8.5 after the news, and Miami's Kalshi price fell from about 40% to 29.5%. Notre Dame won 31 to 24: a push at −7, a Miami cover at −8.5, and a result well inside what the closing price allowed.

What the data says

In 712 games where a statistically defined star did not play, the injured team's price fell only 2.3 points on average, and those teams won 43.1% of the time against 45.6% implied by their final price. The market did not overreact.

Definitions and method

Star player (NFL)
QB: threw at least 60% of the team's pass attempts in that season's prior games, with at least 3 prior games. Skill player: the WR, TE or RB who leads the team in scrimmage yards per game in prior games, averaging at least 70, with at least 3 prior games. Fixed before looking at results.
Star player (NCAAF)
Same rule as the NFL from ESPN box scores: the QB with 60%+ of pass attempts, or the non-passer leading the team in scrimmage yards per game at 70+, each over 3+ prior games. Out = no box-score line in a game his team played, after appearing in one of the team's previous 3 games.
Star player (NCAAM)
In his team's top two for minutes per game and averaging 12+ points, over 5+ prior games played (and at least half the team's games). Out = no minutes in a game his team played, after playing in one of the team's previous 5 games. A box score confirms the absence, not always its cause.
Star-out event (NFL)
The star is listed Out or Doubtful on the final weekly injury report and has no stat line that week.
Price drop (all sports)
The side whose Kalshi price fell between the reference time and the start. Not limited to injuries: it captures any other pre-game news (lineups, rest, starting goalie or pitcher).
Price before the news
6 days before start for NFL and NCAAF, 72 hours for other sports. It must rest on at least 100 contracts traded; if not, the first hour that reaches 100 contracts is used (at least 12 hours before start), otherwise the game is left out. This removes stale opening prices in thin markets, such as a lone 1-contract trade at 97%.
Price
Midpoint of best bid and ask at the end of each hour (last trade if the spread is wider than 10 cents). Start time is Kalshi's occurrence time minus 3 hours, confirmed by the price jump at the start of play.
Edge
Actual win rate minus average price at start. Intervals use the variance implied by the prices.
Coverage
Kalshi single-game markets begin with each league's 2025 season, so 2023 and 2024 are not included. Preseason games and prices under 2 or over 98 cents are excluded.
